## Findings, ordered by importance

### P1 — Shared-brand parent domains still pass the subsidiary identity gate

Locations: `src/orglens/core.py:845`, `src/orglens/core.py:550`, `src/orglens/names.py:76`.

The added weak-proof check uses `domain_matches_name`, which accepts any distinctive shared token. A parent's domain and subsidiary often share exactly such a token. Exact organisation-number proof on a contact/about subpage also bypasses the homepage publisher-label check used by the phone/address corroboration path.

I reproduced a fictional example: homepage `fjordbakst.no` identifies "Fjordbakst Group"; its contact page lists "Fjordbakst Vest AS, Org.nr 123456789". Researching the subsidiary accepts the group's website and publishes the group's LinkedIn account. This directly contradicts the blanket claim that group websites and their accounts are always rejected.

This is a synthetic counterexample, not a finding that any of the 32 actual reviewed sites is wrong. Its importance is that a material wrong-company publication can prevent qualification on a new batch.

Required correction: tie an exact identifier to the site's primary publisher, not merely a listed subsidiary. Add shared-brand parent/portfolio regressions; retain independently verified company-specific domain/section handoff where appropriate. A domain-name match alone cannot supply publisher scope.

### P1 — RSS still admits static pages, unrelated articles and non-article URLs as news

Location: `src/orglens/core.py:895`.

The static-page rule is on the JSON-LD extraction path. `extract_feed` checks title length/date but does not apply that rule, require an individual article URL, or establish that the item is about the target company. Reproductions publish all of these:

- A dated `https://example.no/contact` item titled "Contact us at Example".
- A feed item pointing to an unrelated company's external article.
- An item without an article link, using the feed URL itself as its claimed article URL.

The 300-company release also labels eight Radio Grenland editorial items as company news. Their titles concern local events, a bank, sports and performers. These need a subject-attribution review: being published by the radio company does not automatically make an article news about that company. The remaining three news claims belong to Melbye. Thus the advertised two news-positive companies are not evidence of broad, independently verified company-news recall.

Required correction: one shared news acceptance rule across feed/schema/cards/time extraction, rejecting static pages and missing article URLs, preserving actual publication dates and requiring evidence of company relevance. Independently verify cross-domain article attribution. Do not require the legal name in the headline alone: genuine self-referential company announcements can establish relevance in their body.

### P2 — The NAV cap leaves every job check incomplete despite substantial unused budget

Location: `src/orglens/core.py:1674`; measured artifacts: `submission/release/scale/run-report.json` and `envelopes.jsonl.gz`.

The 300-company run used **2,598/6,000 requests**, leaving 3,402 unused. NAV was capped at 60 feed pages, completed **0/6 segments**, and reported `partial`. Every one of the 300 job availability values is **`failed`**, with its NAV research state `partial`. The smoke and refresh reports likewise stop at 60 pages and 0/6 completed segments.

Therefore, "zero jobs because only 1–2% of random companies advertise" is not established by these tests. Zero verified ads may also occur in a complete source run, but these runs did not establish complete negative checks.

Required correction: allocate the remaining official budget adaptively and complete the feed when affordable. Track source completeness, candidate matching and accepted ads separately. Verify against a small independently identified set of current NAV-positive employers and exact registered subunits. The goal is useful coverage while staying within the shared cap, not spending every request.

Independent live source probe on November 4: with a 120-page allowance and a 140-request safety cap, the same NAV connector completed **6/6 segments using 81 feed pages / 82 total requests in 49.500 seconds**, indexing 8,318 active ads. This demonstrates that the current feed can be completed affordably. It is not a rerun of the historical 300-company batch, does not certify its job recall, and is subject to live source changes. Script/report: `out/review-20261104/nav_headroom_probe.py` and `nav-headroom-report.json`.

### P2 — Numeric social IDs bypass ownership/context checks

Location: `src/orglens/core.py:1092`.

The new `belongs` function returns true for Facebook `profile.php` or long numeric IDs without evaluating credit context. A footer explicitly saying "Website made by agency" with an agency Facebook numeric-profile link is published under the researched company in my reproduction. Non-numeric token matching also needs care with generic business words and geographic names.

Required correction: reject agency/vendor credits, sharing widgets and third-party link groups before applying owner-link acceptance. Preserve positive tests for genuine company-published numeric profile links; do not solve this by dropping every numeric ID.

### P2 — Website research runs before two of its own discovery/verification inputs exist

Locations: `src/orglens/core.py:1607`, `src/orglens/core.py:1645`, `src/orglens/core.py:638`.

The selected balanced phase order runs website research before leadership and NAV. Registered operating-head corroboration cannot use roles fetched later in that run, and NAV employer homepages are collected after the website phase has ended. No bounded second website pass consumes those later hints. This limits keyless discovery even when requests/time remain.

Required correction: consider a bounded second pass for unresolved companies after these verified hints become available. Compare new verified companies and request cost under the exact official budget. Preserve the parent/identity vetoes.

### P2 — Field-name mismatch remains a hypothesis, not the confirmed cause of zero scored external families

Location: `src/orglens/core.py:1361`.

`external_footprint` is a useful explicit view with evidence references. The public evaluation contract describes claims/evidence envelopes, however, and does not state that this new section is what the official parser reads. Starter-kit field names alone do not establish the private evaluator's mappings. Zero official social/news/jobs could arise from extraction, source scope, rejected attribution, execution configuration or ingestion.

Required correction: obtain one rejected/ignored example per family and confirmation of canonical output mapping. Add an evaluator-compatible contract check. Ensure registry activity cannot be counted as external news; a careers link cannot be counted as an active job posting. Ask whether company-published outbound social links meet evidence requirements without fetching the profile itself.

### P2 — Newest-year wording is repaired; newest-year retrieval is not yet demonstrated on the reported failures

Location: `src/orglens/core.py:1289`.

The new summary selects one newest period/account type and omits missing figures rather than borrowing from an older year. It also explicitly distinguishes an older available API period from a newer registered filing. These are correct repairs, covered by meaningful tests.

They do not prove that the 15 organizer-identified companies now return their 2025 figures. The claim that an absent JSON API year means the filing is not published is not established for those cases. Request affected company IDs/source examples and determine whether the newest official document or another official endpoint exposes the values. Preserve an explicit unknown when it does not.

## Secondary points

- The three-year news cutoff and three-word title minimum are local policies, not requirements established by the supplied emails or public contract. They can remove valid facts. Measure their precision/recall effects against reviewed opportunities and the organizer's actual evidence cutoff.
- Registry file modification time is explicitly labelled in the run report, which is better than silently claiming a live fetch. It still is not the source's actual retrieval date. Preserve supplied provenance where available and distinguish file metadata from source retrieval in claim-level evidence.
- The full 300-company report has `registry_snapshot: null`; the small clean-clone bulk check proves basic loading, not full-batch equivalence with Builderr's frozen snapshot. Exercise the full official configuration before declaring that path fully verified.
